# 🦇 Bat Zapper

Are you tired of bats flapping endlessly in your caves, basements, and *perfectly lit* bases?  
Do you wonder how they even got there in the first place?

Worry no more - **the Bat Zapper** is here!  
Just plop it down, and every bat within a 12-block radius will be irresistibly drawn to its mysterious glow…  
…only to be **zapped** into oblivion. ⚡

No more squeaks. No more wings in your face.  
Just peace, quiet, and the faint smell of crispy bat.
